05 — FAQ

Questions?

What happens when I launch?

Your image and metadata are uploaded to pump.fun's IPFS, PumpPortal builds the create transaction, your browser makes the new mint key and your wallet signs. The result is a normal pump.fun coin on its bonding curve, and its stream room on VTUBER opens straight away.

What does it cost?

pump.fun's launch costs (about 0.02 SOL), plus any dev buy you choose and PumpPortal's 0.5% fee on that buy. VTUBER adds no fee.

Who gets the creator fees?

You. The coin launches from your wallet, so pump.fun's creator fees go to you. VTUBER never holds your keys or your coins.

How does my VTuber talk?

An AI model writes each line from the name, bio, traits and catchphrase you gave it, reacting to real trades on your coin. When the model is busy, it uses short house lines instead. Sound is off until a viewer turns it on.

Where do the live trades come from?

PumpPortal's public pump.fun data feed, straight into your browser. If the feed is down, the room says so instead of making trades up.

Can I stream it on pump.fun or Twitch?

Yes. Every room has an overlay link with a transparent background. Add it to OBS as a Browser Source and go live wherever you stream.

Can I change it after launch?

The coin's name, ticker and image are fixed on-chain at launch. The personality lives in the coin's description and a memo on its launch transaction, so design it before you sign.

Which wallets work?

Phantom, Solflare and Backpack. You sign one transaction.

Is VTUBER part of pump.fun?

No. VTUBER is an independent site that launches coins through pump.fun. Only upload characters you own or made. Memecoins can go to zero, and nothing here is financial advice.
